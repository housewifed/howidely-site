Cargo theft has become organized crime.

02 / How it works

The unit acts. Your operator decides.

Everything that can safely happen on its own happens at once. Everything that commits people — police, a tow truck, a call to the driver — waits for a person who can see the whole picture.

  1. 01

    Trigger

    The unit watches the rules you set: geofences, the planned route, stop time, driving time, harsh events, the panic button and its own power.

  2. 02

    Automatically

    It starts recording and streaming the cameras, messages the driver’s phone, and raises an alert in RTAK with a recommended action.

  3. 03

    Operator decides

    The operator sees the truck, its route and live video on one map, and chooses: police with exact GPS and a live video link, roadside assistance, a call to the driver, or dismiss with a reason.

  4. 04

    Safety net

    If nobody responds in time, the alert escalates to a backup operator. Nothing is dropped, and nothing calls emergency services on its own.

    05 / A-BOX Mobile Design targets

    The unit that lives in the truck.

    A fixed Android unit from the team that built A-BOX. It speaks the TAK protocol, so every truck appears in RTAK like any other unit.

    • Hardwired, fixed Android unit
    • LTE, GPS and motion sensors for crash detection
    • Road-facing and cabin cameras
    • Hidden panic button for the driver
    • Backup battery, power-cut and tamper detection
    • Stores events offline, sends them when signal returns

    Design targets, not a datasheet: specifications will change as the unit is built and validated. The cabin camera is used under your company’s driver-privacy policy.
